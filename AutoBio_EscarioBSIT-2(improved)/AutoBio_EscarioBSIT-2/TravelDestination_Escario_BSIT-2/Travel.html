Explore Batanes

Escape to the breathtaking beauty of Batanes, the northernmost paradise of the Philippines. Discover its rolling green hills, stunning cliffs, peaceful beaches, and rich Ivatan culture. Every view feels like a masterpiece of nature.

Top Tourist Spots

Basco Lighthouse

A famous lighthouse offering panoramic views of the ocean, mountains, and the beautiful town of Basco.

Marlboro Hills

Enjoy breathtaking rolling hills where green landscapes meet the blue sea, making it one of Batanes' most iconic destinations.

Valugan Boulder Beach

A unique beach filled with thousands of smooth volcanic boulders shaped by the powerful waves of the Pacific.
